# InAmigos Foundation — Website Feature Highlight Concept Layout

A clean, single-page, self-contained HTML/CSS visual mockup showcasing the **5 essential NGO website features** for **InAmigos Foundation** — a Section 8 non-profit organization based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, founded September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, working across 28 Indian states.

## 🧩 The 5 Essential Features (Numbered 01–05 + Exact "Why This Matters" Notes)

### 01. Donation Section
* **UI Elements**:
  * Prominent **"Donate Now"** CTA button
  * Suggested amount tiles: `₹500`, `₹1,000`, `₹2,500`, `₹5,000` + custom amount input
  * **80G & 12A tax-benefit** messaging (50% tax deduction eligibility with digital certificate emailed)
  * Security badge line: `🔒 100% Encrypted & Secure • UPI, Cards, Net Banking`
* **Why this matters**: *Clear suggested amount tiles remove decision paralysis, while prominent 80G/12A tax exemption messaging provides financial credibility and tangible incentives for Indian donors.*

---

### 02. Volunteer/Internship Sign-up Form ("Become an Amigo")
* **UI Elements**:
  * "Become an Amigo" short form:
    * **Full Name**
    * **Email**
    * **City/Location**
    * **Area of Interest** (dropdown of the six flagship projects)
  * Submit button ("Submit Amigo Application")
  * Note about certificate issuance upon completion
* **Why this matters**: *Low-barrier entry with focused fields (name, email, city, and interest area) allows InAmigos Foundation to rapidly mobilize regional youth and build local ground chapters across all 28 states.*

---

### 03. Impact Showcase
* **UI Elements** (Stat tiles with icons and one-line supporting descriptions):
  * **50,000+ Beneficiaries Reached** — Served hot meals, education kits, clothing, and health support across underprivileged clusters.
  * **200+ Active Volunteers** — Passionate youth and professionals serving as dedicated Amigos in field chapters.
  * **28 States Covered** — Expanding nationwide grassroots footprint and regional community outreach chapters.
  * **6 Flagship Causes** — Integrated programs addressing hunger, schooling, animal rescue, women, climate, and skills.
* **Why this matters**: *Prominently displayed quantitative milestones establish immediate institutional credibility, validating the foundation's nationwide footprint for prospective donors and CSR grant evaluators.*

---

### 04. Flagship Campaigns & Projects
* **UI Elements** (6 cards with real project taglines, category tag, "Learn more →" link, and "Active" status indicator):
  1. **Project Seva (Humanitarian)**: *"Serving humanity with compassion through food and clothing drives for the underprivileged."*
  2. **Project Bachpanshala (Education)**: *"Nurturing young minds and building bright futures through quality education for underprivileged children."*
  3. **Project Jeev (Animal Welfare)**: *"Animal rescue, protection and feeding for strays and injured animals."*
  4. **Project Udaan (Women Empowerment)**: *"Soaring towards a brighter future through skill development and financial independence."*
  5. **Project Prakriti (Environment)**: *"Plant for a better tomorrow — conservation, plantation drives and sustainability."*
  6. **Project Vikas (Skill Development)**: *"Enhancing employability through targeted skill development programs."*
* **Why this matters**: *Organizing initiatives under recognizable flagship project names (Seva, Bachpanshala, Jeev, Udaan, Prakriti, Vikas) gives donors clarity on exact outcomes and lets volunteers align with their specific passion area.*

---

### 05. Testimonials & Beneficiary Voices
* **UI Elements** (3 quote cards with star ratings, quotes, names, roles, and locations):
  1. **Rohit Sharma (Active Volunteer Amigo)**: Project Seva & Prakriti • Raipur Chapter (★★★★★)
  2. **Meena Sahu (Beneficiary's Parent)**: Project Bachpanshala • Bilaspur (★★★★★)
  3. **Pooja Kashyap (Self-Help Artisan Beneficiary)**: Project Udaan Livelihood Center • Chhattisgarh (★★★★★)
* **Why this matters**: *Authentic multi-perspective quotes (from volunteers, parents, and direct beneficiaries) humanize the mission, bridging the gap between statistical impact and real-world human transformation.*
