What We Do

Our Ongoing Projects

Targeted community initiatives driving grassroot empowerment, education, and ecological care.

Project Seva

Providing essential nutritious food, clean drinking water, and warm clothing to the underprivileged and vulnerable families living on the margins.

Project Bachpanshala

Imparting quality primary education, learning materials, and mentoring for underprivileged children to build a foundation for a brighter future.

Project Jeev

Promoting animal welfare through regular rescue missions, emergency medical care, rehabilitation, and compassionate feeding drives for stray animals.

Project Udaan

Dedicated to women empowerment through hands-on vocational training, self-help workshops, and pathways to sustainable financial independence.

Project Prakriti

Fostering environmental conservation, native tree plantation drives, clean-up operations, and community workshops on ecological sustainability.

Project Vikas

Skill development and career orientation programs crafted to enhance youth employability, technical competency, and professional confidence.
